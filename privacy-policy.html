Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Cornu Africae Botanicals respects your privacy and recognises the importance of protecting personal information shared with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and retain information in connection with our website and business activities.

1. Who We Are

Cornu Africae Botanicals is a registered business licensed to export by the Ministry of Trade and Tourism of the Republic of Somaliland. Our business focuses on the wholesale supply and export of natural frankincense resins to international markets.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us or submit a wholesale enquiry through our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Your name and company name.
  • Your email address and other contact details you provide.
  • Your country or intended export destination.
  • Your preferred frankincense type, grade and specifications.
  • Estimated order quantities and commercial requirements.
  • Messages and other information voluntarily provided to us.

Our website infrastructure may also process technical information, including IP addresses, browser information, device information and request logs for security, performance and operational purposes.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use information for purposes including:

  • Responding to business and wholesale enquiries.
  • Preparing quotations and discussing potential supply arrangements.
  • Communicating with prospective and existing business partners.
  • Managing commercial relationships and export arrangements.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing misuse.
  • Understanding website performance and improving our services.
  • Meeting applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

Where applicable data protection laws require a lawful basis for processing, we rely on taking steps towards a contract, legitimate business interests, compliance with legal obligations or consent, as appropriate.

4. Website Analytics and Cookies

Our website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to help us understand website traffic, referral sources, general visitor locations and website performance.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed to provide website statistics without using tracking cookies or persistent identifiers to track individual visitors across websites.

Cloudflare may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to provide website security and operational functionality.

Our website does not currently use Google Analytics or advertising tracking pixels.

Further information about Cloudflare's privacy practices is available at Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party service providers to support our website and business communications, including:

  • Cloudflare: Website hosting, network security, performance and analytics.
  • Resend: Delivery of website enquiry emails.
  • Private Email: Business email hosting and communications.
  • Google Fonts: Delivery of website typography, which may involve processing technical connection information.

Information may also be shared with relevant logistics providers, professional advisers, regulatory authorities or other parties where necessary for legitimate commercial activities or legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information.

6. International Data Transfers

As an internationally focused business, we may communicate with customers, suppliers and service providers located in different countries.

Personal information may therefore be processed outside your country of residence. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we will take appropriate steps to meet those requirements.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, manage business relationships, maintain commercial records and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Retention periods depend on the nature of the information, the purpose for which it was collected and any applicable record-keeping requirements.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not submit passwords, payment card information or other unnecessary sensitive information through our enquiry form.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the data protection laws applicable to your circumstances, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Request portability of eligible personal information.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Submit a complaint to a competent data protection authority.

To exercise any applicable privacy rights, please contact us using the details below.

10. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business operations, website functionality or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
