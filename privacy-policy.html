Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Cornu Africae Botanicals is committed to respecting your privacy and handling personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use and protect information in connection with our website and commercial activities.

1. About Us

Cornu Africae Botanicals is a registered business licensed to export by the Ministry of Trade and Tourism of the Republic of Somaliland, specialising in the wholesale supply of natural frankincense resins to international markets.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal and business contact information when you communicate with us, submit an enquiry or establish a commercial relationship. This may include your name, company, contact details, product requirements and other information you choose to provide.

When you visit our website, certain technical information may also be collected, including IP addresses, browser and device information and website usage data.

3. Use of Information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, administer commercial relationships, facilitate supply and export arrangements, improve our website, maintain security and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where required by applicable law, we process personal information on the basis of contractual necessity or steps requested before entering a contract, legitimate interests, legal obligations or consent, as appropriate.

4. Website Analytics and Cookies

We use privacy-focused website analytics to understand visitor activity and improve website performance. Our analytics service is designed to operate without tracking cookies or persistent cross-site identifiers.

Cookies or similar technologies may be used where necessary for website functionality and security. Where consent is required by applicable law, it will be sought before using non-essential technologies.

5. Disclosure of Information

We may share information with service providers supporting website hosting, security, analytics, website resources and business communications, as well as logistics partners, professional advisers and other parties where necessary to conduct our business or fulfil legal obligations.

We aim to limit disclosures to information reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose. We do not sell personal information.

6. Commercial Confidentiality

We recognise the importance of protecting commercially sensitive information, including product specifications, purchasing requirements and quotation discussions. We seek to handle such information responsibly and limit its disclosure to legitimate business and legal purposes. Any separately agreed confidentiality obligations remain applicable.

7. International Data Transfers

Given the international nature of our business, information may be processed in countries other than your own, including Somaliland and locations where our service providers operate. Where required by applicable law, appropriate safeguards will be applied to international transfers.

8. Data Retention and Security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, taking account of commercial requirements and applicable legal and record-keeping obligations.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, restrict or object to its processing, request data portability, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

Requests concerning your personal information may be directed to us using the contact details below.

10. External Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our business, website or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.
