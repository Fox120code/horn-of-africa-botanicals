Who We Are

Botanicals from an extraordinary land.

Horn of Africa Botanicals is a Somaliland-based botanical ingredients company focused on sourcing and processing some of the world's most distinctive frankincense.

Rooted in the Horn of Africa.

For generations, frankincense has been harvested from the rugged landscapes of Somaliland and the wider Horn of Africa.

We are building a modern supply chain around this heritage — connecting exceptional natural raw materials with international buyers seeking distinctive botanical ingredients.

Our focus is simple: origin, quality, traceability and long-term relationships with our international customers.

Our Frankincense

Distinctive species. Distinctive origins.

We work with premium frankincense varieties from Somaliland's key producing regions.

Maydi / Meydi

Boswellia frereana

A highly valued Somaliland frankincense traditionally associated with the Sanaag and Cal Madow regions. Known for its distinctive resin profile and importance in premium fragrance and botanical applications.

Beeyo / Moxor

Boswellia carterii / sacra

Traditional frankincense sourced from producing areas across Somaliland, including regions of Bari, Sanaag and Sahil. Suitable for applications across fragrance, cosmetics and natural personal-care products.
