Who we are

Exceptional botanicals begin with a meaningful connection to their source.

Horn of Africa Botanicals is a Somaliland-based company focused on sourcing and supplying natural frankincense resin to international buyers.

Our work is grounded in an appreciation for the region's distinctive botanical resources and the people and landscapes behind them. We aim to connect this heritage with today's global fragrance, cosmetic and natural ingredients industries.

As our business develops, we are looking beyond raw materials to the possibilities of local processing, new applications and lasting industry relationships.

Our botanicals

Maydi — The King of Frankincense

Boswellia frereana

High on Somaliland’s rugged limestone cliffs grows one of the world’s most distinctive frankincense trees. Known as the King of Frankincense, Maydi has been treasured for generations for its rich, balsamic fragrance and bright citrus notes. Its extraordinary character begins in a landscape few trees can call home.

Beeyo — An Ancient Aromatic Treasure

Boswellia carterii / sacra

For thousands of years, frankincense has travelled ancient trade routes, filling temples and homes with its unmistakable fragrance. Harvested from the dry landscapes of the Horn of Africa, Beeyo carries that legacy forward with warm, resinous notes, gentle spice and fresh pine.

Beyond the resin

A new chapter in botanical processing.

Frankincense has travelled from this region to the wider world for generations. We believe its next chapter can create more value closer to where it begins.

Horn of Africa Botanicals is in the process of establishing frankincense distillation capabilities in Somaliland, with the aim of producing essential oils for international botanical applications.

Our longer-term outlook embraces thoughtful processing, product development and the exchange of knowledge that makes new possibilities achievable.

Essential oil production is in development and is not currently offered for sale.

Our origin

Shaped by the land. Connected to the world.

Somaliland's dry landscapes and rugged highlands are home to frankincense traditions with deep regional roots. For centuries, natural resins have connected communities here with markets far beyond the Horn of Africa.

We see origin as more than a location. It is an important part of product identity, sourcing knowledge and the relationships that connect natural materials to the people who value them.
